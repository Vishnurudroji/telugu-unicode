THE STORY

Every old document deserves a second life.

Telugu publishing has used legacy font systems for many years. A large amount of existing content is stored using older encodings such as ANU6 and ANU7.

When people try to reuse this content in modern applications, they may face compatibility problems. In many cases, they end up typing the same Telugu text again.

Telugu Vector Studio was developed to make that process easier by providing a way to convert legacy Telugu text into Unicode and convert Unicode text back into supported legacy formats.

UNDER THE HOOD

Simple technology. Purposeful engineering.

The conversion engine uses deterministic mapping rules rather than AI-generated guesses.
